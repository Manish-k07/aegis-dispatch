💡 The Core Research Novelty (Why It Gets Published)

Traditional municipal CAD systems are strictly reactive: an ambulance sits at a static station until a 911 call is placed, leading to fatal delays during traffic peaks. ST-GNN-MARL shifts the paradigm into a proactive, self-optimizing multi-agent network:

1

Dynamic Spatio-Temporal City Graph (ST-GNN)

Models the city road network as a graph $G=(V, E, W_t)$ where edge weights $W_t$ dynamically update based on time-of-day, rain intensity (friction coefficients), and live traffic congestion feeds.

2

Predictive Pre-Positioning via Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MAPPO)

Ambulances are modelled as cooperative autonomous agents. Instead of waiting idly, agents dynamically reposition across high-probability risk clusters before incidents occur, minimizing initial response distances.

3

Pareto-Optimal Multi-Factor Allocation Engine

Replaces simplistic Euclidean/nearest-neighbor matching with a 4-dimensional objective function factoring in road transit ETA, unit capability tier (ALS vs. BLS), and receiving trauma hospital bed congestion.

4

Adaptive Green-Wave Traffic Signal Preemption (V2I IoV)

Simulates Vehicle-to-Infrastructure (V2I) radio signals preempting upcoming traffic intersections 300 meters ahead, creating a virtual green emergency corridor.

📐 Mathematical Problem Formulation

Academic reviewers require clear mathematical definitions. Below is the exact formulation for the paper:

// 1. Multi-Objective Cost Function for Incident-Unit-Hospital Matching:
min J(a_i, e_j, h_k) = w1 * ETA_scene(a_i, e_j, G_t) + w2 * ETA_hosp(e_j, h_k, G_t)
                     + w3 * (1 - CapabilityScore(a_i, e_j.priority))
                     + w4 * (1 / (1 + AvailableICUBeds(h_k)))
Where w1 + w2 + w3 + w4 = 1.0, and G_t is the time-dependent dynamic road graph.
// 2. Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Reward Function for Fleet Repositioning:
R_t = - alpha * sum(ResponseTimes_t) - beta * sum(RelocationFuelCost_t) + gamma * CityCoverageMetric_t
Balances proactive risk coverage against unnecessary fuel/battery depletion.
