**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026  
**Classification:** Enterprise Healthcare & Public Safety Emergency Communications  
**Regulatory Standards:** Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act, General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), National Digital Health Mission (NDHM).

## 1. Executive Summary & Statement of Purpose
Aegis Dispatch CAD ("Aegis CAD", "the System", "we", "our") provides mission-critical computer-aided emergency dispatch software for municipal 911/112 operations, paramedic ambulance fleets, and regional hospital Emergency Departments (ED). We recognize the sensitive nature of emergency healthcare records, telemetry streams, and location services. This Privacy Policy delineates the collection, cryptographic protection, transmission, access control, and statutory archival of all Protected Health Information (PHI) and operational CAD data.

---

## 2. Categories of Information Collected

### 2.1 Protected Health Information (PHI) & Emergency Incident Telemetry
During emergency intake and clinical triage, the System processes:
- **Caller Identifiers:** Name, callback telephone number, and initial reporting timestamp.
- **Incident Location:** Real-time geocoded address, building coordinates, elevation, and road intersection.
- **Patient Clinical Status:** Chief complaint, emergency categorization (Cardiac, Trauma, Stroke, Respiratory), triage priority code (Critical, High, Medium, Low), and triage protocol checklists.
- **Live Streamed Patient Vitals:** Heart rate (BPM), blood pressure (Systolic/Diastolic mmHg), blood oxygen saturation ($SpO_2$), respiratory rate, and continuous telemetry feeds streamed from Mobile Data Terminals (MDT) to receiving trauma centers.

### 2.2 Vehicle & Fleet Telematics
For real-time dynamic routing and proximity calculations:
- Continuous GPS latitude and longitude coordinates.
- Vehicle speed, heading, and ignition state.
- Route progression, estimated time of arrival (ETA), and traffic condition overlays.
- Maintenance diagnostics (engine health, odometer, fuel/battery level, brake wear).

### 2.3 Audio & Communications Data
- Push-to-Talk (PTT) status transmissions, radio broadcasts, and paramedic voice-activated status updates.
- Natural language transcripts generated by local AI clinical triage and dispatcher assistance tools.

## 3. Lawful Basis & Permitted Uses of Data
All collected data is processed strictly under emergency medical care exemptions (HIPAA Treatment, Payment, and Health Care Operations; GDPR Article 6(1)(d) "Vital Interests" and Article 9(2)(c) "Protection of vital interests of data subjects in medical emergencies"):
1. **Emergency Medical Dispatch:** Rapid identification, clinical prioritization, and closest-unit vehicle allocation.
2. **Hospital ED Pre-Arrival Preparation:** Streaming pre-hospital patient vitals to prepare cardiac catheterization labs, stroke teams, or trauma resuscitation bays prior to ambulance arrival.
3. **Smart Traffic Signal Preemption:** Transmitting temporary green wave routing requests to municipal traffic infrastructure.
4. **Regulatory Audit Compliance:** Generating forensic, time-stamped incident audit logs for medical examiner review and legal dispatch verification.

We **do not** sell, monetize, broker, or transmit patient data, caller records, or location coordinates to third-party commercial entities, insurers, or advertising platforms.

## 4. Cryptographic Protection & Data Security

### 4.1 In-Transit Encryption
- All client-server HTTP communications require Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- Live telemetry and streaming WebSocket feeds (`/ws/live`) utilize secure WebSockets (`wss://`).
- HTTP response headers enforce `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-XSS-Protection: 1; mode=block`, and strict `Content-Security-Policy`.

### 4.2 At-Rest Encryption & File Storage
- Persistent databases (PostgreSQL / H2 in PostgreSQL compatibility mode) utilize AES-256 standard database volume encryption.
- Disk snapshots and historical backups generated in `./data/backups/` are restricted to operating system administrator access with sanitized path disclosure protections.

### 4.3 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
The System enforces strict privilege segregation:
- **Central Dispatcher (CAD):** Call intake, fleet tracking, manual/automated dispatches, and system resets.
- **Mobile Paramedic (MDT):** Unit turn-by-turn navigation, waypoint progress updates, and live patient vitals transmission.
- **Hospital Emergency Department (ED):** Bed capacity management, trauma bay availability, pre-arrival patient vitals monitoring, and patient intake handoff.

---

## 5. Data Retention & Archival Policies
- **Active Operations:** Live telemetry is cached in memory for sub-second dispatch latency.
- **Statutory Incident Archive:** Completed emergency records and forensic audit logs are retained in compliance with local emergency communications retention guidelines (statutory duration typically 3 to 7 years).
- **Automated Backup Snapshots:** Nightly and event-triggered JSON snapshots are cataloged with SHA-256 integrity signatures in `./data/backups/`.

## 6. Data Subject Rights & Regulatory Requests
Under applicable health privacy legislation, patients, callers, and authorized legal representatives have the right to:
- Request certified copies of emergency incident logs and dispatch timeline recordings.
- Request correction of inaccurate demographic or medical record entries.
- Inquire about the technical recipients of pre-hospital patient vitals.

Requests may be directed to the municipal EMS Data Protection Officer (DPO) or via our designated compliance terminal:
